Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the North Florida Tamil Sangam app and website experience operated by North Florida Tamil Sangam, a 501(c)(3) organization in Jacksonville, Florida.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Guests can browse public pages without creating an account. If you create an account or send a membership request, we collect the information described below so the Sangam can run the app and serve the community.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Name, email address, and password when you create an account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not stored by the Sangam in plain text.
  • Membership requests. Name, email, phone number, city, household notes, and the membership plan you select when you request membership.
  • Account status. Your sign-in role and whether membership has been marked active after council review.
  • Technical information. Basic device and connection information needed to operate the service securely, such as session tokens and authentication logs.

How we use information

  • To let you sign in and keep your session
  • To receive and review membership requests
  • To show you the public Sangam content in the app
  • To contact you about your account or membership when needed
  • To keep the service secure and working

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. Volunteer council admins may see membership requests and related account status in order to run the Sangam. We use Supabase to host authentication and app data. Supabase processes that information on our behalf so the app can sign you in and store membership requests. Payments made by Zelle are handled by your bank and are not processed inside this app.

Data retention

Account and membership information is kept while your account remains open and as long as the Sangam needs it to manage membership and community records. You may ask us to delete or correct your information by emailing the address below.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can browse as a guest without creating an account. If you have an account, you can sign out at any time. To update or delete your account information, or to ask questions about this policy, contact us.
